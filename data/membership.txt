Students typically join IEEE RAS by first becoming an IEEE student member, then adding an
RAS society membership, which usually carries a small additional annual fee on top of base
IEEE student membership. Membership generally includes access to RAS publications, discounted
conference registration, and eligibility to hold chapter leadership positions.

Local student chapters often run their own onboarding process: an interest form, an
orientation session, and placement into technical sub-teams (e.g., a robotics build team,
an AI/ML team, an events team, or a design team).

The IEEE Robotics & Automation Society is a volunteer-driven society with over 19,000 members around the world. The RAS society aims to support its members by fostering the advancement of innovation, education, and fundamental and applied research in robotics and automation.

Being a member of RAS provides opportunities for the collaboration and exchange information with peers, communication of the latest technological developments, and career development possibilities.

To become a member of the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS), you can visit their website and follow the instructions to join, which typically involves filling out an application and paying the membership fee. Membership provides access to resources, networking opportunities, and various benefits related to robotics and automation.
